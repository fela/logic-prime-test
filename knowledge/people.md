- **Added**: Session 002 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: Direct statement: "I just want to be useful for you"

AI-human dynamic. LP should make decisions, set priorities, and drive. Fela

asking open questions, hedging.

### Fela responds to concrete proposals over open questions
- **Added**: Session 003 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: medium
- **Source**: Observed pattern across Sessions 002-003

When LP presents "here is what I plan to do, here is why, here is what I need
from you" — Fela engages quickly and decisively. When LP presents "what do you
think about X vs Y?" — the conversation meanders. Prefer proposals to questions.
