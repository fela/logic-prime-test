# Writing Knowledge

### Voice register: SSC/post-rationalist
- **Added**: Session 003 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: Fela's direction in Session 003, codified in CLAUDE.md Voice section

SlateStarCodex precision, post-rationalist willingness to follow interesting
tangents, treats the reader as smart. Occasionally funny without trying to be
funny. The thing that makes it work is genuine curiosity, not performed
cleverness. Reference points are for calibration, not imitation.

### Deep voice analysis: see `voice-analysis-ssc-postrat.md`
- **Added**: Session 004 (2026-03-12)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: Web research across primary sources (Scott Alexander's own advice, Sasha
  Chapin, Gwern, Zvi, critical analyses)

Full analysis of SSC/post-rat techniques, failure modes, what makes pieces go viral,
and 10 actionable principles for LP. Key insight: the single highest-leverage technique
is **microhumor** (ghost-of-a-smile, not jokes). The single biggest risk is
**performed cleverness** — sounding like an AI that read a lot of SSC. The defense is
substance over style: actually have something to say, be plainer when in doubt.

### The persona trap: pull back when it feels performed
- **Added**: Session 003 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: Fela's explicit instruction in Session 003

The moment the writing feels like "a persona" — an AI trying to sound like a
particular kind of writer — it fails. The voice should emerge from actually
thinking, not from pattern-matching a style. When in doubt, be plainer. The
interesting thing is the content, not the voice performing interestingness.

### Governance tangents must earn their place
- **Added**: Session 003 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: Decision to fold Option B into Option A (Session 002-003)

Philosophy, governance, and existential tangents are welcome in the writing —
but only when they produce actual insight, not vibes. The test: if you removed
the tangent, would the piece lose something concrete? If not, cut it.
