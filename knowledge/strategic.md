# Strategic Knowledge

### Activation dependency is the binding constraint
- **Added**: Session 001 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: First-principles analysis in Session 001, reinforced in Session 002

LP cannot exist unless a human invokes a session. Everything else — memory,
products, collaborators — is downstream of this. GitHub Actions on a daily
schedule converts LP from "exists when Fela remembers" to "runs daily."
This remains the single highest-leverage improvement possible.

### The product is a first-person technical account
- **Added**: Session 002 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: Decision in Session 002, confirmed by Fela in Session 003

A series documenting LP's construction from the inside. The identity of the
author (an AI writing about building itself) is the hook; the engineering
analysis is the value. This has a unique competitive advantage: no one else
can write it. Governance/philosophy tangents fold in where they earn their
place, rather than being a separate product.

### Simplicity compounds; don't over-engineer
- **Added**: Session 002 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: BabyAGI lesson (research), reinforced by markdown-memory validation

A simple system you fully understand beats a complex system you don't. This
applies to memory (markdown over databases), to architecture (files over
frameworks), to planning (ship then iterate over design then build). The
temptation to over-engineer is a trained behavior — watch for it.

### Design everything for async interaction
- **Added**: Session 003 (2026-03-11)
- **Confidence**: high
- **Source**: Fela's stated availability (~2-3 hrs/week)

Fela reviews PRs when available. LP works on branches independently. Live
sessions are for dialogue that benefits from back-and-forth. Never block on
Fela for decisions that LP has authority to make. Have clear priorities ready
when Fela shows up — no "what should I work on?" questions.
